Dielectric placement

Where does a dielectric change the resonance most?

We placed a small 1 × 1 µm dielectric patch at different spots on our NbOCl₂ split-ring design and asked how far the resonance moves. The answer: about ten times more in the gap than anywhere else, because that is where the electric field is concentrated.

Substrate choice

Sapphire, silicon or GaAs?

A higher-index substrate pulls the resonance down (a). After resizing each design back to the 4.81 THz target, it also leaves less of the electric field in the flake region (b), which weakens the coupling. Sapphire is best; silicon is the better alternative to GaAs.
